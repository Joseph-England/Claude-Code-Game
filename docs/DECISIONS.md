# Decisions log

Numbered and append-only. A decision stands unless a later entry reverses it with a reason.

1. **Title: ALPENGLOW.** The light that stays on the peaks after the sun has gone is the game's central image and its ending.
2. **Stack: Three.js (WebGL2) + Vite, plain JavaScript ES modules.** No TypeScript, to keep iteration fast and build config minimal.
3. **Vite base path is `/Claude-Code-Game/`** (GitHub Pages project site for repo `Joseph-England/Claude-Code-Game`).
4. **Deploy via GitHub Actions** (`actions/deploy-pages`) on every push to `main`; Pages source must be set to "GitHub Actions".
5. **Custom kinematic character controller, no physics engine.** Momentum feel needs hand-tuned friction/drag/steering, not rigid-body behaviour.
6. **Fixed 120 Hz physics step with interpolated rendering.** Deterministic feel independent of frame rate.
7. **Terrain collision = analytic heightfield sampling; all other collision (cave, bridge, rocks, cairns) = `three-mesh-bvh`.** Controller merges both.
8. **Terrain: 2 km × 2 km, 1024² heightfield, generated deterministically at load in a Web Worker** (domain-warped ridged fBm → erosion → route carving via spline SDF).
9. **Surface types: powder, packed, ice, rock**, stored in a splat map aligned with the heightfield; the controller reads friction/drag/control from it.
10. **Terrain rendering: chunked grid with 3 LODs + skirts, height read from a float texture in the vertex shader.** Lets LODs and snow trails share one data source.
11. **One level, 9 sections (0 Opening … 8 Summit), ~1.6 km route, ~5 min.** No additional levels, ever.
12. **Sun elevation is driven by route progress, not wall-clock time** (+12° → −5°), so the sunset always lands at the summit.
13. **Checkpoints are cairns; falling off fades back to the last cairn in < 1.5 s.** No death animations, no lives, no timer pressure.
14. **Story told only through timed text lines in three voices** (The Weight, You, Others). Lines never block input; one on screen at a time.
15. **The ending is ambiguous:** empty summit, more ranges beyond, sun sets, "I'm still here." No victory screen.
16. **Credits include one small support line pointing to findahelpline.com.** No pop-ups or content-warning walls; a brief content note on the title screen.
17. **Moveset: run, variable jump (coyote + buffer), slide, slide-jump, landing projection, wall-kick (from cave onward), rest.** No double jump, no dash.
18. **No hard speed cap on slides; terrain is the limiter.**
19. **Camera: third-person orbit with velocity auto-follow, heightfield collision, speed-based FOV.**
20. **Rendering: HDR half-float target → sky → opaque → particles → custom post chain** (mip-chain bloom, grade, tonemap, vignette/grain, FXAA) built on three's EffectComposer.
21. **Sky: physically based single scattering with transmittance + sky-view LUTs**, artistically pushed toward purple/red/orange.
22. **Snow: custom shader** (wrap/SSS diffuse, violet sky-lit shadows, glitter, triplanar detail, surface blending) plus ring-buffer trail render target displacing powder.
23. **All audio is procedural Web Audio**, including reverb impulse responses. No sample files.
24. **Avatar is an abstract procedural figure with a verlet-simulated red scarf.**
25. **Quality tiers Low/Medium/High, auto-picked by a 2 s benchmark, plus dynamic resolution on Low/Medium.** Medium targets integrated GPUs at 60 fps.
26. **Controls:** WASD/arrows move, mouse look (pointer lock), Space jump, Shift slide, Esc pause. Gamepad: left stick, right stick, A jump, B/RT slide, Start pause.
27. **Dev-only `lil-gui` tuning panel and stats overlay**, stripped from production builds via `import.meta.env.DEV`.
28. **Claude owns all GitHub work (user request, Session 1).** Each session develops on its assigned branch, opens a PR to `main`, waits for the `build` check to go green, merges it (merge commit), and confirms the Pages deploy run on `main` succeeds. If the branch's previous PR is already merged, restart the branch from `origin/main` first. The user does not need to merge manually.
29. **The workflow builds every PR to `main` (build job only; deploy runs only on pushes to `main`).** Gives a CI signal before merging.
30. **Heightfield collision samples a Catmull-Rom bicubic surface and its exact gradient** (refines #7). Bilinear height with blended normals disagreed at edges, so bodies stuck to cliffs and lips never launched; with one C1 surface, crest launches follow real curvature (v²/R > g·cosθ).
31. **Gravity is 15 m/s² (not 9.81).** Snappier jumps and faster slope acceleration for a 5-minute game; drag values are tuned against it.
32. **Debug overlay (F3) and lil-gui tuning panel (F4) ship in all builds until Phase 6** (reverses the "stripped from production" half of #27). The user playtests on the Pages link and needs them there; Phase 6 gates them behind `import.meta.env.DEV` or a URL flag.
33. **Shadows: `PCFShadowMap` with `shadow.radius` (Vogel-disk soft PCF), normalBias, and a light frustum that follows the player, snapped to shadow texels.** three r186 removed `PCFSoftShadowMap` (it silently fell back to hard PCF), which plus a 600 m frustum at 2048² and a 7° sun caused the blocky striped shadows on the Phase 1 mountains. Phase 4 cascades keep this setup per cascade.
34. **Final Phase 2 movement constants are the values in `src/tuning.js` at the end of Session 2**, with measured behaviour and rationale in DESIGN §2 "Tuning". `npm run check` guards them; change a constant and the check shows what moved.
35. **Camera terrain avoidance lifts the boom (pitch) before pulling in; vertical follow is a lead-compensated critically damped spring.** Instant pull-ins and a saturating lag clamp caused visible snaps and bumps (the user's "choppy/bumps" note).
36. **Play heightfield is 1024 m × 1024 m at 1 m cells (1025²), plus a render-only backdrop of distant ranges (14 km, 125 m cells)** (refines #8). The route fits in ~700 m, and 2 m cells blurred the features the controller needs (half-pipe walls, 2 m kickers, a 6 m ridge crest). Generation is ~2.2 s in the worker.
37. **The route climbs ~150 m (trailhead 0 → summit 150), not ~550 m** (refines DESIGN §3). Measured climb rates (5.2 m/s run on 20° packed → 1.8 m/s vertical) put 550 m of climbing alone at ~5 min. The summit is made to read as the top with a cone cap on the terrain and the backdrop ranges beyond it.
38. **Macro shape = inverse-distance blend of the route's own heights + a cone rising toward the summit + domain-warped ridged noise that grows with distance from the route**, then erosion, then the route is carved in by cross-section profiles (trail, basin, pipe, ridge, cave, plateau, summit). The mountain is built around the route, so no hand-sculpting is needed.
39. **Route heights come from per-section knots smoothed over ±5 m; "hard" knots stop the smoothing** so steps, kickers, the ridge-to-cave cliff and the crevasse stay sharp.
40. **The chute-2 crevasse (7 m) has no rock detour.** Everyone reaches the lip fast (ice can't be stopped on at 14°: grip 0.1), the lip launches a slide automatically and a running jump also clears it; a miss costs one respawn at the cairn before chute 2.
41. **Route trimmed to 1106 m with the summit 102 m above the trailhead** (supersedes the numbers in #37). A headless bot playthrough measured 4:06 for the first layout (≈ 7 min first-time); after trimming: bot 3:05, first-time estimate ≈ 5:30, in line with DESIGN §3's section table (which sums to 6:00). The valley falls to −150 m at the map edge, so the summit still stands ~250 m over its surroundings.
42. **Wall-kick chimneys use the Phase 2-verified layout (back panel 3.5 m before the step face, doorway at one side), not a slot between side walls.** The side-wall slot failed twice (you hit the step face before reaching a side wall); two-strike rule → switched to the layout the movement check already proves.
43. **Controller/collider bug fix: resting contacts register (0.03 m skin) and terrain snapping never pulls the body down through a collider floor.** Box tops within snap distance above terrain made the player sink into boxes (the chimney step blocks). Not a tuning change: `npm run check` numbers are unchanged.
44. **Momentum banks get a rock strip at one edge** (rock grips to 55°), so a player who hasn't learned to slide can still walk up (DESIGN §2: "a struggling player can still walk everything except the chutes' big gaps"). The Foot bank is 1.6 m over 2.4 m for the same reason: a standing-start running jump only worked in a 0.5 m window.
45. **Out-of-bounds is per section: falling a set depth below the route (measured with crevasses bridged), straying a set distance sideways, or standing on the ice-cave roof.** Respawn fades out 0.35 s, holds 0.15 s, fades in 0.5 s (1.0 s total). R returns to the last cairn at any time, and the game hints it after 12 s without progress.
46. **CI runs `npm run playthrough`** (bot completes the route, all cairn respawns stable, momentum gates clear from rest, soft-lock sweep ≤ 3 bot misses). About 3 s.
47. **Shift sprints, C / right mouse slides; walk 5 m/s, sprint 9 m/s, unlimited stamina** (amends #26, user playtest). 7 m/s was too fast for a walk and too slow for a sprint. Gamepad: B/RT slide, L3/LB/LT sprint. Slide-jump physics are unchanged; `npm run check` now measures walk and sprint separately, and the playthrough bot sprints (never inside chimneys).
48. **Harder route, 5 checkpoints, every surface has a job** (user playtest: "3:15 with zero struggle", "too many checkpoints", "a stroll up a hill", surfaces not used meaningfully). Checkpoints: trailhead, chutes, ridge, cave floor, summit push (was 14); the whiteout cairns A/B/C and the summit cairn are story cairns only (`[s, d, 'note']`). Surfaces: **rock** = grip and shelter (the only way up the new 46° summit couloir is a zig-zag rock line — step onto the snow and you slide back to its foot; gusts × 0.35 on rock); **ice** = speed without grip (chutes; three near-flat ice patches on the ridge you must cross between gusts on momentum or jump); **powder** = drag (the packed trail through the fields is narrower, 1.2 m, and swings wider). Ridge narrower (w 2.4) with stronger, more frequent gusts (8.5 m/s², every 4.6 s); crevasse 8.5 m (a sprint jump no longer clears it; slide-jump does). Summit now 117 m above the trailhead. Bot 2:48; first-time estimate 5:55 with the new retry costs.
49. **Every HUD text element sits on a soft dark backing; the three voices look different in kind** (user playtest: white text unreadable on snow; voices felt like "several people in my head"). The Weight is dim italic lowercase at screen centre on a smoky blot with a dark vignette pressing in (it reads as the depression, not a person); You is plain warm text in the lower third; Others are amber hand-lettered notes captioned "a note tucked into the cairn". Phase 5's narrator keeps these styles.
50. **Procedural avatar: capsule figure in a joint hierarchy with a distance-driven gait, pose blending (stand/run/slide crouch/air tuck/sit) and a 10-point verlet scarf; lean capped at 0.08 rad on foot and 0.3 rad in a slide, scaled 0.35 × atan(a_lat/g)** (user playtest: lean was far too strong when running and turning the camera, since camera-relative input turns the velocity and the old lean took up to 0.6 rad from it; the "blob" was the placeholder capsule squashed to 45 % in the rest-sit pose, now a real sitting pose with knees up).
51. **Post chain is hand-rolled full-screen passes (three's `FullScreenQuad`), not `EffectComposer`** (refines #20): HDR half-float target with a depth texture → atmosphere → bloom → composite (grade, AgX tonemap, vignette, grain, speed effects, sRGB) → FXAA. Fewer intermediate targets, and the fog pass needs the depth texture anyway. MSAA off (FXAA only), as DESIGN §6 lists.
52. **Sun path: +12° at the trailhead → +0.8° on arrival at the summit (progress^1.15), then under the horizon to −5° over ~40 s after arrival** (refines #12). At −5° there is no direct light left for the climb itself; the sun touching the horizon as you top out is the better beat, and the ending (Phase 5) owns the rest of the sunset. Sun azimuth 112° (west-south-west): behind you on the ridge (long shadows ahead), into the sun on the summit push, and setting ahead of you from the top.
53. **Terrain shadows are ray-marched on the GPU into a 1 m sun-visibility map (soft penumbra = min clearance/distance), recomputed in 4 strips when the sun moves ≥ 0.15°; the terrain casts nothing into the cascades.** Cascades (three's CSM, 2 × 1024 on Medium, fade on) carry only props and the avatar, so the shadow pass is a handful of draw calls and there are no culled-caster holes from sun-side chunks outside the view. Ambient is the sky-view LUT sampled toward the normal (π × radiance, violet push included) plus snow bounce; the hemisphere light is gone.
54. **Snow is three's standard material with patched chunks, not a from-scratch shader** (DESIGN §4): splat-blended albedo/roughness, triplanar analytic-noise detail normals (sastrugi on crust), a custom direct BRDF (wrap diffuse + blue terminator subsurface + GGX + hashed micro-facet glitter), Fresnel sky reflection on ice/crust. Keeping three's light loop keeps CSM, fog-free depth and the shared world lighting working with no duplicated shadow code. The distant ranges use the same shader with slope-based rock.
55. **Snow trails: 128 m toroidal ring buffer (512² on Medium = 25 cm texels), ping-ponged by one full-screen pass per frame that clears scrolled-in texels and stamps the path capsule + gait footprints.** Vertex displacement only on LOD 0 (powder sinks 0.3 m × depth, crust 0.06 m), with the full-resolution gradient bending normals so footprints show between 1 m vertices; the avatar is lowered to match. Trails behind you fade out after ~64 m (window edge), not "for the whole section" — a bigger window costs resolution.
56. **Fog, aerial perspective and whiteout are one post pass over the depth buffer, not per-material fog.** One code path covers terrain, backdrop, props, avatar and particles; in-scatter colour comes from the sky-view LUT toward the horizon in each view direction. Aerial extinction is Rayleigh-weighted at ~2 km altitude and pushed ×5 so the 1 km mountain and the 14 km ranges read at their scale. Particles write depth (alpha-tested) so they fog correctly.
57. **Particles are three instanced draw calls: procedural snowfall (camera-wrapped box, wind field in the vertex shader), procedural spindrift off the ridge/summit crest, and one CPU-emit/GPU-integrate ring-buffer pool (slide spray, landing puffs, breath, cairn embers).** Alpha-tested/dithered with depth write (fog-correct). Snowfall density follows a per-section table (arc.js), the whiteout adds a streaked 11 m/s blizzard along the route, and the sky is clear after the storm.
58. **Grade is a per-section table (saturation, temperature, contrast) blended across section joins; exposure opens up ×3.2 as the sun goes under; the alpenglow is an extra red-violet irradiance on surfaces above a height that climbs as the sun sinks.** Bloom is a 5-mip (Medium) COD-style chain mixed at 5 %, Karis-averaged on the first mip. Speed (14→30 m/s) adds radial streaks and rim chromatic aberration.
59. **Quality tiers change live except the shadow cascade count, which is fixed at startup** (it is compiled into every lit material; rebuilding CSM + recompiling all programs mid-game isn't worth it). The benchmark runs once (first visit, 2 s after load) and stores its pick; `?quality=` and F2 override and are remembered. Dynamic resolution uses GPU time from `EXT_disjoint_timer_query_webgl2` when available (vsync hides headroom from frame time), frame time otherwise; range 0.5 → tier scale.
60. **Wall-kick removed from the game** (reverses the wall-kick part of #17; user playtest: it no longer fits the theme, and the chimney walls and step blocks looked out of place). The controller, tuning, panel and movement check lose it; the gray-box course keeps its walls as plain geometry. The Summit Push chimney is now a rock ramp.
61. **The collapsing bridge and ice cave are replaced by "The Descent"** (reverses the collapse in DESIGN §1/§3; user playtest). The ridge runs out and the only way on is a long packed slope down into a sheltered hollow (a checkpoint cairn at the bottom), then the path climbs out: going down to get back up, a choice rather than a fall. Slide it and the speed carries you part of the way up the far side. 130 m, 46 → 22 → 49 m.
62. **No summit flag; whiteout cairns spread out; section 8's end trigger starts the ending** (user playtest: the flag blocked the view; the three notes came too close together). Whiteout 110 → 165 m, notes at 28 / 82 / 138 m (~15 s apart walking into the headwind), and each note stays up while you stand at its cairn.
63. **Tighter ground control** (user playtest: "slippery"): run accel 10 → 22 (walk reaches 90 % in 0.36 s), brake 9 → 24, turn accel 28 → 70, skid brake 14 → 30 from 105°, air control 0.15 → 0.3. Ice keeps its job by staying loose (control 0.22, grip 0.05: an 8 m stop from a walk). Slides, jumps and launches are unchanged. `npm run check` targets updated to match.
64. **Avatar redesign, no idle animation, cloth scarf** (user playtest: primitive figure, unbalanced idle, scarf "flies all over the place"). The figure is a small climber: lathed jacket with hem and collar, pack with bedroll, beanie with pompom, mittens, boots; the stance is upright over the feet and standing still is still (no breathing bob or sway). New poses: lying in the snow (the opening), getting up through a sit, and a reach down to leave a stone. The scarf is a 10-particle position-based strip at a fixed 240 Hz with drag toward the air's velocity (it streams back in proportion to speed through the air), bend limits and a chest/pack sphere. The old one moved only the child in each distance constraint ("follow the leader"), which pumps energy into a swinging chain: that was the flailing.
65. **Backdrop ranges are structured, not a field of spikes** (user playtest: the mountains did not look like mountains). 193² grid (73 m cells, was 125 m) over 14 km; warped ridged noise lays out the main crest lines, an "eroded" fBm (each octave damped by the slope of the octaves below it) adds spurs, gullies and cols, a power curve keeps valleys broad and summits sharp, a low-frequency mask makes some ranges higher, and the ranges start ~1.7 km out and reach ~2 km above the valley. +46k triangles (one draw call).
66. **Fog and haze below the horizon take the sky colour from higher up as the sun sets, and the height fog's sun glow needs a lit sun** (user playtest: the sunset glow seemed to come from the valleys). The valley fog is in shadow at sunset, so it is dimmer and bluer than the horizon band; the glow now stays in the sky.
67. **Flow: the title sits over the live scene; the run starts lying in the snow.** Loading progress shows on the title; once the mountain exists the title turns translucent over a slow low camera beside the figure lying at the trailhead, looking up toward the summit. Click (or Enter/Space) starts: audio unlocks, pointer locks, "stay down. it's easier." appears, and nothing moves until the first input, which lets that line go, fires "Get up." and plays a 2.6 s get-up; the controls strip shows for 16 s after. Summit → ending (control removed) → credits → click or 40 s → title with a fresh run. `?spawn=N` skips the title and opening (dev). The "R" reminder shows at most once per 90 s after 20 s truly stuck, never on the summit; the first checkpoint explains itself once and later ones are embers only.
68. **Inner-voice lines are final and locked (DESIGN §1): 30 lines, down from 34.** One revision pass for restraint: cut lines that narrated what the player could already see or feel, and the draft's "look how far there is to fall" (it points at the drop, which the care rules forbid); the relapse pair is rewritten for the Descent; the wind answer becomes "It sounds like me. It isn't." Conditional lines fire on real events (first input, first jump, first packed step, speed, a fall in the chutes, the first strong gust, reading a note, leaving a stone); two of them also fire at the end of their stretch so every player gets them; "slow" and "retry" only fire for players who struggle.
69. **Audio is one procedural graph built at the first click** (DESIGN §5): master → compressor; two generated stereo impulse responses (a 4.5 s open-air tail for music and voices; a 1.4 s dark "sheltered" room that opens up in the Descent's hollow and in the whiteout — it replaces the cave reverb); wind = looped pink noise through a rumble (altitude, whiteout), a resonant whistle band (air speed, altitude, a random drift), a hiss (whiteout, speed) and a gust band panned toward the gust; footsteps are 4 pre-rendered variations per surface (packed = a burst of highpassed micro-clicks, powder = soft lowpassed compression, ice = tick + scrape + glint, rock = thud) fired from the gait's foot plants; a slide hiss follows speed and surface; breaths on jumps, hard landings, effort (powder, sprinting) and slow ones while sitting; FM bells under the lines. The score (`music.js`) has 10 moods (9 sections + the climb out of the hollow): detuned triangle/saw pads through a lowpass with 3.5 s swells, and a sparse FM glass motif with Poisson timing and short rising phrases; D minor → F/Bb → an unresolved D add9 ↔ G maj7 at the summit. Levels checked with an analyser in headless Chromium (≈ −30 dBFS RMS pre-compressor while walking, peaks < 0.15).
70. **Notes are read by standing at the cairn; the stone is an action.** A note appears when you come within 4.5 m of its cairn and stays up while you stand there (up to 30 s), then fades when you walk on. After the third note, "E · leave a stone" (X on a gamepad) appears under it; pressing it crouches the figure into a reach, a stone lands on top of the stack with a knock and a few embers, the note lets go and "I'll leave one too. For whoever's next." follows. Walking on without it is allowed, and the line simply never comes.
71. **The ending is a fixed 55 s timeline from arrival, and the sun stops at −3°, not −5°** (refines #52; user playtest: it was unclear what to do at the top). Walking onto the summit (12 m in) takes control away: the figure walks to the sit spot and sits, then slowly turns its back on the sunset to face the far ranges. Camera: blends from the gameplay camera over 3 s, lifts and pulls back toward the setting sun (range after range, 4–17 s), then swings ~160° round behind the figure to look east at the peaks still lit (18–38 s). Lines at 1.5 s (26), 12 s (27 + 28), 28 s (29), 41 s (30, with the score's resolve); fade at 50 s; credits at 55 s. At −5° the scene was nearly black; at −3° with exposure opening up to ×6 the Belt of Venus, the stars and the alpenglow read. Spindrift stops in the ending (it drifted across the lens).
72. **Pacing (Phase 5): ≈ 5:54 title to credits for a first-time player (climb ≈ 4:54, opening ~8 s, ending 52 s); lines re-spaced so none waits behind another.** `npm run playthrough` now replays the narrator's timing at first-time pace (trigger times × estimate/bot) and fails if a line waits > 6 s (direct answers and the stone line excepted). Moves: line 3 to 20 m, line 5 to the powder's edge, line 6 armed from 22 m, line 17 at 4 m, note A at 36 m, line 22 at 10 m; the ending's lines at 1.5 / 11 / 26 / 38 s. The climb is kept at ~5 min rather than cut to hit exactly 5:00 overall: the ending is the payoff and needs its minute. Soft-lock sweep: 3 bot misses (limit 3), one new at the Descent's left shoulder (s 826, d −16): a hollow off the path; R and the stuck reminder cover it.
73. **Footsteps are synthesized from a heel-then-ball force curve per surface (`audio/steps.js`), with variation on every play; gusts are a roar and a rush with turbulence; the mix puts steps lower** (user playtest, Session 6: packed too high and not crunchy, powder repetitive, rock "a toy drum", gusts poor, steps a little loud). Packed = dense damped cracks in 0.4–2 kHz plus a low body (the Phase 5 highpassed clicks had a 16 kHz spectral centroid; now ~1.1 kHz); powder = low compression, muffled crunches, a spill after; rock = noise knock + grit + toe scuff, no oscillators (the landing's sine thump is gone too). 8 variations per surface (3 heavier for landings), never the same twice in a row, each play varies rate ±6 %, level ±1.7 dB, pan toward the foot and a ±3 dB presence EQ. Levels are normalised per surface (K-weighted 50 ms peak) and steps now sit ~7 dB below Phase 5's packed steps. Gusts: a deep lowpassed roar and a broad rush with per-frame turbulence; the envelope swells from the 0.8 s warning, pans from upwind and decays over ~2 s. The storm adds a two-band howl, and its roar is heard from the approach. Measured in Node with the same K-weighting and a spectral centroid (functions in steps.js), not by ear.
74. **The gait plants the feet: a phase-driven foot trajectory per leg (stance: the foot stays where it landed; swing: lift and carry) solved with two-bone IK against the heightfield under each foot** (user playtest: in a walking game the walk must satisfy). Cadence 1.9 + 0.32·v steps/s; stance share chosen so a planted foot travels ±0.30–0.42 m about the hip (a walk when slow, a jog with flight at 5 m/s, a run at 9); toe-off heel lift; only planted feet hold the hips down; hips ride steadily lower on descents; torso leans into climbs; feet lift higher in powder; turning on the spot is done in small steps; heel strikes fire the step sound (panned to that foot), the footprint and a kick of powder. `tools/check-gait.mjs`: 0.000 m/s planted-foot slip on flat and ±20° slopes at 2/5/9 m/s, 2–5 cm hip bob.
75. **No sitting** (user playtest: the sit was awkward; at the top you should stand and look). The automatic rest-sit at cairns is removed (reading a note at a cairn used to sit you down after 3 s), and the ending walks the figure to the viewpoint, where it stands facing the sunset, lifts its head a little, and at 23 s turns (stepping) to the alpenglow as the camera swings round (refines #71: its timeline and camera are otherwise unchanged). The get-up in the opening still passes through a sit.
76. **Scarf collides with capsules for the jacket, pack, bedroll, head and upper arms; contact is inelastic; a reset settles it outside the body first; the ribbon's width lies across the strip** (user playtest: it still clipped through the figure). The single chest sphere left the pack, bedroll and shoulders uncovered. Pushing a particle out of a collider no longer turns into velocity (85 % of the push is removed), which stopped the strip being flung upward after a respawn. The knot sits on the back of the collar. `tools/check-gait.mjs` measures ≤ 5 mm inside the true body surface walking, sprinting, turning and in side/head/tail winds, with the tip hanging below the knot at rest.
77. **The whiteout is a wind gap: a col between two rock horns, and the storm is a place** (user playtest: the blizzard came from nowhere and vanished when you left it). Real blizzards like this happen where wind is funnelled through a gap and speeds up, tearing snow off the flanks, so terrain-gen raises horns either side of the section (~45 m above the path, 50–100 m out, tallest mid-gap, below the summit) and a `col` profile climbs from the path into them. The storm is 12 soft vertical cylinders along the col (`world/storm.js`); the fog pass integrates their density analytically along each view ray (1 − r²/R² along a line is a cubic), thins it above the col, streams it with noise and lights it with sky and sun, so from the climb out of the hollow you see a pale plume in the notch, inside visibility is ~16 m, and looking back from the summit ridge it is still there. The player's whiteout value, headwind (building to 20 % on the approach), snow density and streaks, and the audio all come from the same field; it fades over ~40 m after the col instead of at a section line.
78. **Summit Push is a walk up the summit ridge** (reverses the couloir and rock line of #48 and the Summit Push bank; user playtest: the final ascent was anticlimactic and looked bad; drop platforming ambitions, this is a walking game). Out of the storm onto a broad shoulder (trail, 8 m) that narrows into a ridge (4 m crest, 34 m falls) at a steady 20–25°, 62 → 117 m, with the ranges on both sides and the low sun ahead; rock breaks through at the ridge's edges; breathing gets heavier on steep climbs. The playthrough drops the Summit Push momentum gates. Level progress now also catches up when you rejoin the bed up to 120 m ahead (a slide along a shoulder could leave it frozen, which the sweep exposed).
79. **The camera never rolls; the title is centred on both axes** (user playtest, Session 7: the carve roll tilted the horizon whenever turning while moving, which was disorienting and fought the views). Roll is gone entirely (camera and tuning). The title's heading was wider than its 560 px column (large letter-spacing) and overflowed to the right, and the column was pushed 18 vh down; the column is now viewport-sized and centred, the note keeps its own measure.
80. **No packed snow** (user playtest: it served no purpose, and its crunchy step sounded bad). Surface 0 is now plain "snow": the old packed physics (firm footing, so walking and momentum numbers are unchanged), but it looks exactly like snow (same albedo/roughness/glitter as powder, no sastrugi or sheen) and sounds like snow. Deep powder stays where it means something (the Powder Fields, the whiteout col): slower, deeper prints, knees higher.
